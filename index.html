Features

Built for glass, tins, toys, coins, pottery, and whatever you collect.

Whether you're keeping a collection or selling at shows and online, every item gets a clear record from the day you buy it to the day it sells.

Snap it, log it

Take a photo and record what you paid, the condition, where it's stored, and whether you're keeping or selling it.

Your real profit

Enter the sale, selling fees and shipping. See what you actually made after what you paid. Asking prices never count as earnings.

Find anything fast

Search by name, category, condition or storage spot. Browse a photo grid or a list, filtered by Keeping, For Sale or Sold.

Ready for the show

Build a Show List with asking prices and a total for antique shows and flea markets, and record sales right at your booth.

Year-end summary

Pick a year to see whether you're up or down, then export a spreadsheet with one row per sale to share with your tax preparer.

Synced and backed up

Your collection syncs privately through your own iCloud to all your devices, with one-tap or daily backups to iCloud Drive.

  • Data stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud.

Everything you enter, including item names, descriptions, photos, prices, storage locations and sales, is stored on your device. We cannot see or access it.

If iCloud sync is on (it is by default), your App data is also stored in the private database of your own iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit service, so it appears on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. This data is held by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy and your iCloud terms. Only you can access it; we cannot. You can turn sync off in the App's Settings or in your device's iCloud settings.

The App uses the camera only when you tap to take a photo of an item, and reads your photo library only through Apple's photo picker, for the photos you select. Photos are stored with your item records as described above.

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The App checks with Apple, on your device, whether you have purchased Unlimited Items.

Because we hold none of your App data, you are in full control of it. How do I move my collection to a new phone?

With iCloud sync on (the default), just sign in to the same Apple ID on the new phone and open the App; your items sync over. You can also go to Settings > Backup & Restore on the old phone, tap Back Up Now, then restore that backup on the new one.

My items aren't showing up on my other device.

Make sure both devices use the same Apple ID, iCloud is on for the App, and you have free iCloud storage. Open Settings in the App to see the iCloud Sync status. Sync can take a few minutes, especially with many photos.

How do I restore my Unlimited Items purchase?

Open Settings > Unlock Unlimited Items and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We aren't able to issue refunds ourselves.

Can you recover my deleted items?

No. We never receive your data, so we can't see or restore it. If you have a backup in iCloud Drive or the Files app, you can restore it from Settings > Backup & Restore.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the App, then remove its data from iCloud: on iPhone, open Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage, choose Collector's Ledger and delete its data. Delete any backup files you saved in iCloud Drive or Files.

Is the Year-End Summary good enough for my taxes?

It's a record to share with your tax preparer, not tax advice. It only knows what you entered, so please review it with a qualified tax professional.
